## Unresolved in `spec.md` — too vague to verify (awaiting decision)

- **G1 — Warning behavior for unreadable/undecodable files.** `spec.md` lines 87–88, 97, 107 say “optional per-file warnings” and “a warning to stderr” but do not specify required text, format, or whether stderr must be non-empty. “Optional” warnings on exit 0/1 also make stderr assertions indeterminate. Decide exact required stderr behavior.
- **G2 — Trigger for exit 4.** `spec.md` lines 91, 99, 107 require exit 4 for “unexpected internal error” without defining how to induce one or where the boundary with exit 3 lies. Decide the fault-injection method and the required message content beyond “plain-text” and “no traceback.”
- **G3 — Skipped files and score normalization.** `spec.md` lines 70–71 define `top` as the highest count “among all candidate files,” while line 97 says unreadable/undecodable files are skipped. Decide whether skipped files count toward `top`; this changes expected scores.
- **G4 — Exact no-match stdout bytes.** `spec.md` line 79 requires “one line followed by a newline,” while line 81 says no-match stdout is “exactly `[]`.” Decide whether exit-1 stdout is `[]` or `[]\n`.
- **G5 — Scope of “reads only” / “does not access.”** `spec.md` lines 10, 13, 48 say the program reads only the knowledge base and does not access files outside it, but the program necessarily reads its own source and the standard library. Decide whether this means data-file content reads only, or also metadata/stat access, and how to audit it.
- **G6 — Accepted `--max-results` forms and argument order.** `spec.md` lines 27–32 and 46 say “integer `N >= 1`” but do not settle `+5`, `05`, `1_0`, whitespace, huge integers, or whether options may precede the query. These change exit 2 versus exit 0.
- **G7 — Cleanup scope.** `spec.md` line 107 requires “all temporary test fixtures are removed afterward” but does not say whether cleanup is required after a test failure or interruption, or what counts as a fixture. Decide the cleanup obligation.

I stopped before resolving G1–G7; no decisions were invented.
